SUNLIT VALLEY EXPANDED 1.2

More Adventure, More Risk, More Reward

Built on Society: Sunlit Valley 4.1.5A by Chakyl and contributors. Thank you for creating the foundation of this pack.

[Visit the original SSV pack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/society-sunlit-valley)

WHAT'S NEW

- Dragon's Den encounters and rewards.
- Death's Due and Guild recovery.
- SVE quests and 22 Guild bounties.
- Butchery, Metallurgy and spell integration.
- Ink, Runic and Sculk slime integration.
- Collector's Caravan quests and trades.
- Expanded Perfection milestones.
- Recipe, shipping and travel balancing.
- More netherite retexturing to fit Iridium theme.
