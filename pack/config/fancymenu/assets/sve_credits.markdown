# The Valley

Sunlit Valley Expanded

SVE adaptation, integrations and custom development: Drambuie89.

## SVE artwork

Updated SVE loading-screen, main-menu and icon artwork: spacelizards and Reevy.

## With thanks to Society: Sunlit Valley

Built on Society: Sunlit Valley by Chakyl and the Society: Sunlit Valley team. Thank you to the developers, artists, builders, translators and community contributors whose work gives this valley its life. The farming, fishing, community and economy at its heart come from their original pack.

SVE adds its own adventure, progression and balance changes. For the original cozy experience, please visit and support Society: Sunlit Valley. 

For SVE-specific questions, visit The Valley's project page.

SVE is a custom adaptation, not an official release from the SSV team. Individual mods and assets retain their own authorship and licenses.

Original modpack and SVE project links:

[SSV](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/society-sunlit-valley)

[SVE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/the-valley-expanded)
